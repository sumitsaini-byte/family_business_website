Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Find answers to common questions about our furniture rental services.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 weeks in advance for small events and 1-2 months for large events to ensure availability.

Do you provide delivery and setup?

Yes! We provide free delivery, setup, and collection for all bookings within our service area.

What if something gets damaged during the event?

Minor wear and tear is expected. For significant damage, we charge reasonable repair or replacement costs. We also offer optional damage protection.

Can I customize my rental package?

Absolutely! We create custom packages based on your specific needs and budget. Just let us know your requirements.
